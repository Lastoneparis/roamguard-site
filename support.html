RoamGuard support

Most questions about RoamGuard are really questions about what iOS lets a data-usage app see. Those are answered first.

Write to us

contact@oshi-messenger.com

Include your iPhone model, your iOS version, whether you use one SIM or two, and the two numbers that disagree — RoamGuard's and your carrier's. That is almost always enough.

The numbers disagree

This is the most common report, and it is usually not a bug. RoamGuard counts from the moment it was installed; your carrier counts from the start of your billing cycle. If you installed mid-cycle, RoamGuard is missing everything before that.

The fix is one step: open RoamGuard, tap the pool that is wrong, and enter your carrier's current figure. RoamGuard realigns to it and carries on from there. Do this once after installing and once at the start of each cycle if you want it tight.

Troubleshooting

An alert did not arrive
iOS Settings → Notifications → RoamGuard: allow notifications, with sounds. For the 100% alert to break through a Focus, Time Sensitive must be permitted.
The widget shows an old figure
iOS decides how often a widget refreshes. Opening the app updates it immediately.
The cycle reset on the wrong day
Set your cycle start date in RoamGuard's settings — it defaults to the 1st, which is not every carrier's billing day.
My second line is not tracked
Both lines must be active in iOS Settings → Cellular. RoamGuard shows a pool per line that iOS reports as enabled.
Restoring a purchase
Settings → Restore Purchases, on the Apple Account that bought it.

Privacy, briefly

RoamGuard has no account, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no location permission, and it makes no network requests of its own. Deleting the app deletes everything it held. The full text is in the privacy policy.
