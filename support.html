Support

Email hugomoriceau@icloud.com and you will get a reply. Please include your iOS version and what you were doing at the time.

Why does my carrier's number differ from RoamGuard's?

iOS gives apps no way to read your plan's usage. RoamGuard measures the traffic this iPhone carries, starting from the moment you installed it — so if you installed it partway through a billing cycle, everything before that is not counted. Carriers may also zero-rate certain traffic or round differently.

Open Settings → Match my carrier's figure, enter the number your carrier shows, and RoamGuard realigns its total and continues from there. Doing this once per cycle keeps the two in step.

Why do I have to tell it when I am roaming?

Because iOS does not expose roaming status to apps at all. There is no public API for it — CTCarrier, the only thing that ever reported the serving network's country, was deprecated in iOS 16 and now returns placeholder values.

So RoamGuard treats your own switch as the truth. In Settings → Roaming you can set Home or Roaming explicitly, or leave it on Automatic, which infers roaming from your device's region. The app always tells you which of the two produced the answer, so a guess is never presented as a fact.

Does it count my Personal Hotspot?

Yes. Data used by devices tethered to your iPhone is counted, because your iPhone genuinely carries it — and your carrier bills it.

Will it drain my battery?

No. RoamGuard has no timers and never polls. It reads the counter when you open the app, when the network changes, and when iOS grants a background refresh. Because the counter is cumulative, a missed refresh loses nothing.

The numbers stopped updating

iOS decides when apps may run in the background, and it may stop granting refreshes to an app you rarely open. Opening RoamGuard always takes a fresh reading and catches up on everything since the last one.

Do my alerts reset each month?

Yes. Alerts are scoped to the billing cycle, so an 80% alert that fired last month fires again in the next one.

Can I keep my history if I change iPhone?

Turn on Settings → Sync history with iCloud. History is kept per device, so an iPhone and an iPad on one account are never added together.

How do I export my data?

On the History tab, tap the share button and choose CSV or PDF.
