2. What the app does, and its limits

RoamGuard estimates mobile data usage by reading the operating system's network interface byte counters.

These estimates are not a bill. Your carrier's own figure is the one that gets charged, and it is the authoritative one.

You acknowledge and accept that:

  • iOS provides no API that reports actual plan usage, so the app's figures are an independent measurement rather than your carrier's accounting.
  • Measurement begins when the app takes its first reading, which may be partway through your billing cycle.
  • iOS provides no API that reports roaming status. Whether traffic is counted as home or roaming comes from your own setting, or from an estimate based on your device's region.
  • Your carrier's accounting may differ: some traffic may be zero-rated, rounded differently, batched, or measured at a different point in the network.
  • Data used by devices connected to your Personal Hotspot is included, because your phone carries it.
  • iOS controls when apps may run in the background, so measurement intervals may be long and the record of which network was active during them may be incomplete.
  • Traffic carried between the app's last reading and a device restart, or between the last reading and a cellular context being torn down, cannot be recovered.

The developer is not liable for any charges, overage fees, throttling or disconnection arising from reliance on the app's figures. Always treat your carrier's own figure as authoritative.
