Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Summary

RoamGuard collects no personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no third-party SDK. Everything the app measures stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

All of the following is stored only on your device, in the app's own container and its app group (shared with the RoamGuard widget and Apple Watch app):

  • Your plan settings: billing day, allowances, alert thresholds, units, theme.
  • Daily totals of mobile and Wi-Fi data measured on this device.
  • Any figures you enter when calibrating against your carrier's number.
  • A record of which alerts have already been delivered, so they are not repeated.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. RoamGuard makes no network requests of its own for any purpose, including updates, crash reporting or diagnostics.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit or sell any data.
  • It does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising or attribution SDKs.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not request or use your location.
  • It does not read the contents of your network traffic. It reads only the operating system's byte counters — how much data an interface has carried, never what that data was.
  • It does not require or offer an account.

System APIs used

RoamGuard's privacy manifest declares every required-reason API it accesses:

APIReasonWhy
System boot time35F9.1The kernel's byte counters reset when the device restarts. Detecting a reboot prevents the app from misreading that reset as several gigabytes of traffic.
User defaultsCA92.1Stores your settings and shares them with the RoamGuard widget and Watch app through the app group.
Disk spaceE174.1Checked when you export a CSV or PDF, so the export fails cleanly rather than producing a truncated file.

Permissions

RoamGuard requests exactly one permission: notifications, so it can warn you before you exceed your allowance. Declining it leaves the rest of the app fully functional; you simply will not receive alerts.

Data you export

CSV and PDF exports are created at your request and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where they go from there is entirely your choice. RoamGuard does not upload them.

iCloud

iCloud sync is off by default and entirely optional. If you turn it on, your usage history is stored in your own private iCloud database, which only you can access — the developer cannot see it. Turning it off returns the app to device-only storage. If you enable an encrypted device backup, that backup is governed by Apple's terms, not by RoamGuard.

Children

RoamGuard does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page and the date above will change.
