What iOS does and does not allow

Read this before you rely on it

iOS does not hand an app your carrier's usage figure, and it does not tell an app whether you are roaming. No app on the App Store can do either.

So RoamGuard measures the data this iPhone carries, starting from the moment you install it, and lets you set your roaming state yourself. Whenever you check your carrier's own number, type it in once and RoamGuard realigns to it.

Your carrier's figure is the one that gets billed. RoamGuard exists so that figure never surprises you.

Questions

Why is RoamGuard's number different from my carrier's?
It counts from installation, and carriers round and batch differently. Enter the carrier figure once and RoamGuard realigns to it.
Does it track my location?
No. RoamGuard never requests location permission. You tell it when you are roaming.
Does it send my usage anywhere?
No. The app makes no network requests of its own. There is no account and no analytics.
Two SIMs?
Each line is charged to its own home or roaming pool, so a work eSIM and a personal SIM do not contaminate each other's figures.
