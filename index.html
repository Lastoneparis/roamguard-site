Honest about what it can and cannot do

iOS does not give apps your carrier's usage figure, and it does not tell apps whether you are roaming. RoamGuard measures the data this iPhone carries, starting from the moment you install it, and lets you set your roaming state yourself. Whenever you check your carrier's own number, enter it once and RoamGuard realigns to it. Your carrier's figure is always the one that gets billed — RoamGuard is here to make sure it never surprises you.
